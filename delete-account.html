Delete your account

Snakes & Ladders – Haavu Eni (app ID com.haavueni.snakesladders), made by Haavu Eni Games.

Option 1: inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open the game and tap the settings (gear) button.
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Yes, delete.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to onlineplayer2204@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Haavu Eni account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your name, email, sign-in, and game totals (games, wins, coins, language, last played). Online room data is removed when rooms close.

What is kept

Anonymous daily totals (for example "120 games played today") and payment records (product, price and date, with no name or email) are kept for accounting. Subscriptions are managed by Google Play: deleting your account does not cancel the ₹45 monthly plan. Cancel it in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
