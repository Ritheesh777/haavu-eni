Privacy Policy

Snakes & Ladders – Haavu Eni (app ID com.haavueni.snakesladders)
Last updated: 30 October 2026

This game is made by an independent developer in Karnataka, India. We respect your privacy. This page explains what data is used and why.

1. Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or location. We have no accounts and no servers. Your coins, unlocked boards, settings and saved games are stored only on your own phone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and usage information to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Read how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled fully by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the game sets a once-a-day reminder on your phone. It is created on your device; nothing is sent to us.

5. Sharing

When you tap "Share", the game creates an image of your win and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes. We do not receive it.

6. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all game data on your phone.
