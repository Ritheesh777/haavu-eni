Privacy Policy

Snakes & Ladders – Haavu Eni (app ID com.haavueni.snakesladders)
Last updated: 31 October 2026

This game is made by an independent developer in Karnataka, India. We respect your privacy. This page explains what data is used and why.

1. Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or location. We have no accounts. Your coins, unlocked boards, settings and saved games are stored only on your own phone.

Play with Friends (online): only if you use this mode, the game sends the nickname you type, your dice moves, the emoji reactions you tap and a random anonymous player ID to Google Firebase (Realtime Database, servers in Singapore), so that the other players in your room can see them. This anonymous ID is not linked to your name, phone number or Google account. Room data is deleted when the last player leaves the room, and is otherwise overwritten when the room code is used again. Read how Google Firebase handles data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and basic device and usage information to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Read how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" monthly subscription is handled fully by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or payment details. You can cancel any time in the Play Store (Payments & subscriptions).

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the game sets a once-a-day reminder on your phone. It is created on your device; nothing is sent to us.

5. Sharing

When you tap "Share", the game creates an image of your win and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where it goes. We do not receive it.

6. Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes all game data on your phone. To ask us to delete online room data sooner, email us with your room code.
